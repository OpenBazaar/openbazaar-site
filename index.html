Get the app

Your own shop. No fees. No one to shut it down.

OpenBazaar is a free app for selling from your own storefront and buying straight from independent sellers. Buyers pay in Bitcoin, and with escrow the payment is held until the order arrives.

For sellers

Sell without a platform in the middle

Big marketplaces take a cut of every sale, can hold your payouts, and can close your shop after a policy change. On OpenBazaar there’s no company account and nobody taking a percentage.

No fees on your sales

No listing fees, no percentage of each sale, no monthly plan. The only cost is the normal Bitcoin network fee when coins are sent.

No account to suspend

Your shop belongs to recovery words that only you hold, not to an account at a company. There’s no review team that can freeze it overnight.

Paid straight to your wallet

Each buyer pays a fresh address in the wallet built into your app. No platform balance, no payout schedule, no reserve held back.

A storefront that’s yours

Your name, logo, banner, about page and shipping and returns policies, without building or hosting a website.

What your shop can do today

  • Sell physical goods or digital downloads
  • Up to eight photos per listing, plus sizes, colors and other options
  • Set prices in dollars, euros, pounds or yen. Buyers pay the bitcoin amount when they order
  • Shipping prices and the countries you ship to
  • Coupon codes
  • One sales screen for what’s paid, what to ship and what’s done
  • Mark an order shipped with the carrier, tracking number and a note
  • Reviews from verified buyers, which you can reply to

For buyers

Buy directly from independent shops

Your order goes to the seller, not through a platform, and the whole price goes to them. The protections below come from how payment works, not from a company deciding to take your side.

Your money waits until it arrives

When a shop offers escrow, choose it at checkout and your payment is locked until you confirm the order arrived. The seller can’t take it before then, and if they never ship, it comes back to you after the agreed time.

Your address goes to the seller only

Your order is sealed so only that seller can read it. There’s no platform building a history of what you buy, and this site has no trackers.

Reviews from people who paid

Only someone who bought the item can review it, and the seller can reply but can’t edit what they wrote.

How an order works

Four steps, and a plan for when things go wrong

  1. Order

    Pick something from a shop, add your address and choose escrow at checkout.

  2. Pay

    Pay the bitcoin amount from the app’s wallet or any other wallet. With escrow it’s locked, so neither side can take it.

  3. Ship

    The seller sees the paid order, ships it and adds the carrier and tracking number to your order.

  4. Confirm

    When it arrives, confirm the order. That releases the payment to the seller, and you can leave a review.

If something goes wrong

The package never arrives, or isn’t what was described
Open a dispute before the deadline. The moderator you picked at checkout chooses between the two outcomes you and the seller agreed to up front: your money back, or paid to the seller.
The seller goes silent
If the seller never ships and nobody acts, the payment returns to you after the time agreed at checkout.
The buyer got it but never confirms
Sellers can open a dispute too, and the moderator can release the payment to them.
What the moderator can’t do
Take the money, change the amounts or pay anyone else. They only pick one of the two payouts you both signed. Their fee is set in advance and only applies if they have to rule.

Escrow works on the test network today. It stays off for real bitcoin until the payment system has had an outside review. You can also pay a seller directly, which is quicker but has none of this protection.

Honest answers

The things you’d want to know before you install anything.

Can I use it for real sales today?

No. This is an early test version (alpha 3). It runs on Bitcoin’s test network, where coins are free and worth nothing. Use it to set up a shop, place test orders and tell us what’s missing. Don’t send real bitcoin to it.

Who else is on it?

Very few people so far. The app comes with three demo shops so you can try a whole order, plus whatever shops other early testers have opened. If you open a shop now, you’re one of the first.

Do buyers need bitcoin? Can they pay by card?

Payment is in Bitcoin, from the wallet built into the app or any other Bitcoin wallet. There’s no card or PayPal checkout. During the test you use free test coins from a testnet4 faucet.

What does it cost?

The app is free, and OpenBazaar takes nothing from any sale. You pay the normal Bitcoin network fee when coins move. If an escrow order goes to a dispute, the moderator’s fee, set in advance, comes out of the payout.

How will buyers find my shop?

Buyers browse and search the marketplace inside the app, and can follow a shop directly. There are no ads or promoted listings, so early on your best customers are the ones you bring with you.

Does my computer have to stay on?

Your shop takes orders while the app is running. To keep it open around the clock, run the home version on a spare always-on computer and manage it from your laptop or Android phone.

Can buyers and sellers message each other?

Messages inside each order are built and coming in the next release. In this version the seller can add a note for the buyer when they mark an order shipped.

What if I lose my computer?

Your shop and wallet come from a set of recovery words, not a company account. Write them down and keep them somewhere safe. There’s no company that can reset them for you.

Is there a phone app?

There’s an Android app for managing a shop that runs on a home computer, installed from the file below rather than Google Play. There’s no iPhone app yet.

Version 0.1.0 alpha 3

Try it today, with test coins

One app does everything. Open it and your shop is open, quit and it closes. There’s no sign-up.

Open a shop

  1. Download the app for your computer below.
  2. Set a wallet password, then choose “Sell”.
  3. Add your shop’s name, logo and policies.
  4. List something with photos and a price.

Try buying

  1. Download the app for your computer below.
  2. Set a wallet password, then choose “Shop”.
  3. Get free test coins from a testnet4 faucet and send them to the app’s wallet.
  4. Place a test order from one of the demo shops.

Your computer will warn you the first time These test builds aren’t signed yet. On macOS, open the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose “Open Anyway” (on older macOS, right-click the app and choose Open). On Windows, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. On Linux, make the AppImage executable with chmod +x first.
